# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator to calculate Simple Interest based on the Principal Amount, Rate of Interest, and Time Period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest
- T = Time Period

## Example

If:

- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

## Features

- Calculates simple interest easily
- Simple and easy-to-understand formula
- Useful for basic financial calculations
